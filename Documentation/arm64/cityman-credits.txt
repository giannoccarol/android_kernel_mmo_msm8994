Lumia 950 XL (cityman) kernel: credits
======================================

This branch (lineage-18.1-cityman-rom) is the Android4Lumia950 commonized
MSM8994 kernel with Lumia 950 XL changes on top. Work from others that it
uses, and where it comes from:

Base
----
- Android4Lumia950 (djokcho, EpicLPer, j0sh1x and the community):
  android_kernel_mmo_msm8994, the talkman/cityman device trees, the
  charging, USB and Bluetooth UART fixes it already carried.
- Code Aurora Forum / Qualcomm: the MSM8994 kernel, msm_thermal (KTM), BCL
  and the default KTM limits.

Audio (speaker, earpiece, calls)
--------------------------------
- EpicLPer (Android4Lumia950, talkman): the TAS2552/TAS2553 speaker
  amplifier driver and the Quaternary MI2S wiring (e4d0c025066,
  9bd1bb032d4, 22039ccd1ea, 5b52570b88d), and the AP-side sensor I2C buses
  (7d96420fc1e).
- shadowofleaf96 (github.com/shadowofleaf96/android_kernel_msft_cityman):
  the cityman Q6 MI2S node, the unused WCD9330 speaker supplies and the
  ASM open without an ACDB topology (efb1ebf, 1a51144), the MSM8994 eMMC
  clock rates (4bc7ab2), the sensor and NFC includes for cityman (cd01dc9,
  386e7a0) and the nq-nci resume fix (628c06f).

Eight cores and thermal protection
----------------------------------
- Huawei/Google, angler (Nexus 6P) and LineageOS, OnePlus 2
  (LineageOS/android_kernel_oneplus_msm8994): the MSM8994 KTM limits and the
  eight-core bring-up order used as reference.
- Sony (sonyxperiadev, Kitakami): the A53/A57 sensor map and the A57 steps
  used as reference.
- shadowofleaf96: the only other cityman port running eight cores, used for
  comparison; nothing of it is copied here.

Crash logs (pstore)
-------------------
- hraj9258 (Himanshu Raj) and sonic011gamer
  (github.com/hraj9258/android_kernel_mmo_msm8994, talkman-cam-clean):
  the ramoops region at the top of DDR bank 1 and the pmsg bounce buffer
  allocated once (b32f68bcb4b, cherry-picked as is). On cityman the board
  override that moved ramoops elsewhere is dropped so it uses that region.

Not in this branch
------------------
- archienz (github.com/archienz/android_kernel_mmo_msm8994): rear camera
  measurements on the Lumia 950 (CSI lane map 0x0423, 24 MHz MCLK), used on
  the lineage-18.1-cityman-camera branch.
- hraj9258 (github.com/hraj9258/android_kernel_mmo_msm8994,
  talkman-cam-clean): talkman rear camera bring-up, under evaluation for
  cityman (only the pstore commit above is taken).
